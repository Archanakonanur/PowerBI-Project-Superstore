# PowerBI-Project-Superstore

This repository contains a Power BI report designed to analyze and visualize the sales and profitability performance of a company across various segments, products, and regions. The report uses historical sales and profit data to provide actionable insights that can guide business decision-making.

## Overview

The Power BI report focuses on key performance indicators (KPIs) such as:
- **Sales Growth Rate**
- **Profitability Growth Rate**
- **Segment Contributions**
- **Sales and Profit by Region and Product**
- **Discount Strategy Analysis**
- **Product Mix Evaluation**

### Key Insights:
1. **Growth Rate Analysis**: The report identifies trends in sales and profitability growth, with a focus on understanding why growth may have slowed in recent years.
2. **Segment Contribution**: The **Consumer segment** is identified as the largest contributor to total sales, with a detailed breakdown of profit margins and opportunities for improvement.
3. **Discount Policy Review**: The report analyzes the impact of discounts on profitability, identifying products sold with discounts over 20% as incurring losses.
4. **Sales Decline in Specific Regions**: The decline in sales in the **Virginia** region is highlighted, with recommendations for further investigation.
5. **Product Mix and Profitability**: The report identifies high-margin products and suggests investing in marketing and sales efforts for these products while considering the discontinuation of low-margin products.

## Data Sources

- The data for this analysis was sourced from https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/vivek468/superstore-dataset-final, which includes historical sales data across multiple regions and product categories.
- The dataset includes information on sales, profit, discounts, regions, and product categories, which is used to generate visualizations and insights in the Power BI report.

## Requirements

- **Power BI Desktop**: The report file (`.pbix`) is built using **Power BI Desktop**. 
- **Data Source**: The report uses data in **CSV format**

## Instructions

1. **Download Power BI Desktop** if you don’t have it already.
2. **Open the PBIX file**: Download the `PowerBIReport.pbix` from this repository and open it in Power BI Desktop.
3. **Refresh Data**: If you want to update the data, ensure you have access to the same dataset or replace it with your own data source in the **Data** tab.
4. **Review and Interact**: Once the report is open, you can interact with various visuals (e.g., sales trends, profitability charts, region maps) to uncover insights.

## Notes

- The report is designed to provide **high-level insights** and **detailed performance breakdowns** across different time periods (monthly, quarterly, or yearly).
- Adjust the **time period slicers** (Year, Month, or Quarter) to focus on specific periods and analyze growth or performance fluctuations over time.
- You can add **filters** and **slicers** to customize the view of the data based on different segments, regions, or products.
